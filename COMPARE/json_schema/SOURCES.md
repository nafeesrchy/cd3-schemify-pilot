### Parse notes

- **Variable identity:** column names are unique across all sheets; `_v1`/`_v2` suffixes mark the two visits on `bloods` and `omics`, `_p2` marks the second questionnaire on `questionnaires`, `_hs`/`_hc`/`_os` mark the three haemoglobin devices on `Hb_measurements`, `_ntg` marks the Nightingale biomarkers. **`basicInfo` lists `identifier` ("unique anonymous identifier", `X`) — a participant key exists**, unlike INTERVAL.
- **Group rows:** `pica_1 – pica_7`, `rls_6a – rls_6g`, `rls_7a – rls_7g` (checkbox groups, requested), and `importance1 - importance5`, `preference1 - preference3` (ranking groups, not requested) expanded in `VARIABLES.csv` to one row per column. The codings name `Pica_1 : 1 = Ice` (capital P) while the row's name is `pica_1` — the schema will use the row's name.
- **Rows with no variable name** (not inventoried): `questionnaires` row 2 (a "PLEASE READ" note, below), `bloods` row 97 (a data row: description "Time of appointment.", Visit 2, `HH:MM:SS` — a variable whose name is missing; not requested), `omics` row 3 ("Other Linked data:"), `EHRData` row 6 (footnote about the Caliber dataset and `CenDate`). The `*NHSBT codes for adverse events` footnote is also skipped.
- **The note on `questionnaires` row 2:** "As more questions were added after recruitment started, some participants were asked to complete a second questionnaire (see BasicInfo-CommsCode variable). All others received one questionnaire with all the questions. The blue cells reflect the time and date of completing the second questionnaire as well as the variables from these additional questions." `commsCode`: `CP1 = Participants were asked to complete two separate questionnaires, CP2 = Participants were asked to complete one full questionnaire`. The **blue cell colours** that mark which variables came from the second questionnaire are formatting, not text; the `_p2` variables are only the start/end date and time.
- **Marker column** (`&`, `#`, `*`, `^`, `& # :5.`, `& ^ :10.`): no legend in the file. On `Hb_measurements` `&`, `#`, `*` fall on the three devices (Haemospect, Hemocue, Orsense); on `bloods` `&` marks visit-1 rows and `#`/`*` visit-2 rows; `^` marks time-of-appointment rows. Undocumented; not carried into the schemas.
- **Value codes seen (all four sheets with codes):** `999 = don't know / prefer not to answer` on 71 rows, `777` as a top-coded value ("777 > 190kg", "more than N") on 20, `998 = not applicable` on 12 rows (`Breathless_scale`, `preference1 - 3`, `rls_3`, `rls_4`, `rls_5`, `rls_8`, `rls_9a`, `rls_9b`, `rls_10`–`rls_13`). No dataset-wide missing-value convention is stated.
- **Routing scan verdict:** signals seen — `998 = not applicable` with no universe stated; "(vegetarians only)" on `ageVeg`; "If so" on `rls_9b`; `commsCode` (CP1/CP2) and the `_p2` variables (a stated universe: only participants who completed a second questionnaire); gate-and-detail families (`ironSuppPres`/`ironFreqPres`, `ironSuppMulti`/`ironFreqMulti`, `ironSuppOnly`/`ironFreqOnly`, `alc*`/`sm*` if present); no branching-logic column, no questionnaire among the inputs.
- **Ranges on the `ht` and `wt` rows** sit in the Categories cell as text: `0 - 2.99 metres as given`, `38 -190 kilograms as given (777 > 190kg)`.
